It started with one child

One morning, on his way to work, David Kitavi met nine-year-old Mwajuma. She was crying, hungry and alone. She had been beaten and chased from home.

He took her in, found her a home with a grandmother who was already caring for four orphans, and gathered a little money from friends to rent one room in Kibera: a safe place where children could play and learn.

He left his job to run it full time. There were four children. Ushirika has been growing ever since, one child at a time.

From PP1 to senior school, in one place

Ushirika follows Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC). A child who joins us in PP1 can stay with us all the way to Grade 12.

  1. Pre-primary

    PP1–PP2

    Early reading, numbers, play and routine.

  2. Primary

    Grade 1–6

    Strong foundations in literacy, numeracy and science.

  3. Junior School

    Grade 7–9

    A wider range of subjects as students prepare for senior school.

  4. Senior School

    Grade 10–12

    CBC Senior School, where students follow a chosen pathway.

More about learning at Ushirika

More than lessons

Many of our children face hunger and hardship at home. These programmes help them stay in school and grow in confidence.

  • Feeding programme

    1,600 meals a day, so children can learn on a full stomach.

  • Girls' programme

    Mentoring and support that help girls stay in school and keep learning.

  • Mentorship

    Guidance on school, life and what comes after the final exam.

  • Clubs

    A place to explore interests beyond the classroom and make friends.

  • Sports

    Team games that build fitness, discipline and confidence.

  • Music and drama

    Singing, performing and storytelling that help children find their voice.
